## 📋 PDF Specifications Implemented

### 1. Navigation & Brand Identity (Page 2)
- **Official Logo**: Displayed in Navbar, Hero, About Us, Application Modal, and Footer.
- **Navigation Bar**: `Home` | `Study in Turkey` | `Universities` | `Our Services` | `How It Works` | `About Us` | `Contact`
- **Primary CTA**: `[ Start Your Application ]`
- **Secondary CTA**: `[ Talk to an Advisor ]` / WhatsApp

### 2. Home Page (Page 2)
- **Title**: *"Study in Turkey. Build Your Future."*
- **Subtitle**: *"Your trusted partner for university admission, visa assistance and student support in Turkey."*
- **Hero Image**: High-quality Istanbul / campus visual with the Future Link logo clearly visible.
- **Why Students Choose Future Link (5 Core Services Table)**:
  1. **University Admissions**: Find the right university and program.
  2. **Visa Assistance**: Guidance throughout your Turkish visa process.
  3. **Accommodation**: Get help arranging suitable student accommodation.
  4. **✈️ Airport Pickup**: We’ll help you get settled when you arrive.
  5. **Student Support**: Banking, insurance, SIM and other settlement assistance.

### 3. Study in Turkey (Page 3)
- **Headline**: *"Your Future Starts in Turkey"*
- **Why Turkey**: Internationally recognized universities, English-taught programs, affordable tuition options, vibrant student life, strategic location between Europe & Asia, international student community.
- **Interactive "Find Your Program" Tool**:
  - Study Level: *Bachelor's • Master's • PhD • Language School*
  - Field: *Business • Engineering • Medicine • Nursing • Pharmacy • Computer Science • Architecture • Aviation*
  - Language: *English • Turkish*
  - Action Button: `[ Find My University ]`

### 4. Universities (Page 3)
- **Featured Benchmark Profile**: **Istanbul Okan University**
  - City: *Istanbul*
  - Degrees: *Bachelor's & Master's*
  - Students: *International Students Welcome*
  - Languages: *English & Turkish programs*
  - Profile features: Introduction, campus, location, programs, tuition fees, admission requirements, deadlines, accommodation info, and `[ Apply through Future Link ]` button.

### 5. Start Your Application Portal (Page 3)
- **Personal Information**: Full Name, Date of Birth, Nationality, Country of Residence, Father Name, Mother Name.
- **Contact**: WhatsApp Number, Email.
- **Education**: Highest Qualification, Graduation Year, GPA / Grade.
- **Study Preference**: Study Level, Preferred Field, Preferred City, Preferred Language.
- **Documents**: Passport, Certificate, Transcript, Other Documents (with instant file upload simulator).
- **Confirmation Message**: *"Thank you! Your application has been received. A Future Link advisor will contact you shortly."* + Auto-generated Application ID (`FL-XXXXXX`).

- **Our Mission**: To make international education simpler, more accessible and more supportive for students.

- **Our Values**: *Integrity | Student First | Professionalism | Transparency | Growth*.
